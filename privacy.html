Privacy notice

General Traders · Delivery & Distribution app

Who this is for

This app is used by the staff of General Traders (drivers, helpers and the office). It also keeps the details of the shops we deliver to. This page says what the app keeps, why, and who can see it.

What the app keeps about staff

  • Name, mobile number, role, joining date and work details given by the office.
  • Punch in and punch out time, and the vehicle KM you type.
  • Your photo (selfie) when you punch in.
  • Your location, only at the moment you tap a button: Punch In, Punch Out, Arrived, Delivered, Problem, or Save shop location. The office can also press "Locate now" while your app is open.
  • Your delivery work: which shops you delivered, goods returned, and problems you reported.
  • Advances and cuts from salary, entered by the office.

The app does not follow you in the background. It does not read your location when it is closed or when you are not tapping one of the buttons above.

What the app keeps about shops

  • Shop name, owner phone number, address, GST number and the map location of the shop.
  • The bills (invoices) in the daily bill file and what was delivered or returned.

Why we keep it

  • To plan routes and deliveries, and to know what was delivered.
  • To mark attendance and work out salary, advances and cuts.
  • To keep staff and vehicles safe on the road, and to solve problems with a delivery.
  • To meet business, tax and legal record needs.

We use the data only for these work purposes. We do not sell it and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own attendance, advances and deliveries.
  • Other people on the same delivery plan can see which shops are marked as delivered.
  • The office (admin) can see staff, shop and delivery records and live location when you use the app.
  • Our technology providers (the website host and the database service) store the data for us. They do not use it for their own purposes.
  • We share data with others only if the law requires it.

How it is kept safe

  • Passwords are stored in a scrambled form, not as plain text.
  • Every person signs in with their own mobile number and password. A driver can open only their own records.
  • Photos and files are kept private. The connection to the website is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Please do not share your password. Tell the office at once if you lose your phone.

How long we keep it

We keep records while a person works with the company and for as long as needed for salary, tax and legal records. After that they are deleted or made anonymous.

Your choices

  • You can ask the office to show you the details kept about you.
  • You can ask the office to correct anything that is wrong.
  • You can ask questions or raise a concern about your data at any time.
